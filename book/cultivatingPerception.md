Peers understanding how complex a task is.
The group need to appreciate and understand each others time and achievements. The outside world is of little consequence. The organisations approval is of little consequence.
* The problem is all new young people will envisage VR, AI, MMORPG's, Hacking. They don't get exited about 'binary to hex conversion'

* [Born Against, or why hobby programming communities are aggressively against LLM usage](https://blog.fogus.me/llm/born-against.html)
    * > the process of mastering a difficult field itself is the product, and something that runs is generally a nice-to-have.
    * > respect is earned slowly through years of activity in their respective fora, sharing elegant code, displays of genuine curiosity, and through sharing deep domain knowledge along the way. At the end of the day, these communities don’t care if your code works at all, but instead care that you know why and how it works.
    * > Using an LLM to generate the finished piece doesn’t make us craftsmen; it just robs us of the craft.
